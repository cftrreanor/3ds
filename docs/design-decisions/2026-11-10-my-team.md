# My team (2026-11-10)

Owner request (issue #272): one place for hosts to see and remove everyone on the account's team.

Before the panel, the owner set: the removal ladder (Account Host removes co-hosts, Volunteer Leads, and
Coordinators; co-hosts remove Volunteer Leads and Coordinators; Volunteer Leads remove Coordinators on
their events; anyone can leave; nobody removes the Account Host); remove per event or from all events;
invite co-hosts from this page.

Full panel on mock-ups. Screens in docs/design-reviews/2026-11-10-my-team-mockups/.
