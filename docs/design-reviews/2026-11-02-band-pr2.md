## The consultant's verdict

Ship after fixes. Publishing says exactly what will happen, and Get the word out is the right first
thing after it. The weak spot is "opens later": badges, buttons, and invitations still act as if
registration is open, or opens on publish. Make "opens later" a real state everywhere.

## Blocks people (severity 3–4)

### 1. "Opens later" isn't a state of its own · severity 3 · 8 of 8
- **Where:** event page Band registration card (green "Registration open" + "Close registration"), bands page (Close next to "Opens Aug 15"), draft card ("opens when you publish"), the share message after "Open registration"
- **Suggested fix:** an "Opens Aug 15" badge everywhere; no Close before opening, "Change opening day" instead; draft card names the day
- **Decision:** _pending_

### 2. Invitations can't go out before opening day, and nothing brings the organizer back · severity 3 · 7 of 8
- **Where:** Get the word out (opens later), invite tab
- **Suggested fix:** schedule the chosen invitations for opening day, or send now with the opening date, or at least email the organizer that day
- **Decision:** _pending (owner)_

### 3. Setting an opening day on a live contest locks out registered bands · severity 3 · Software Engineer
- **Where:** Registration opens field after registration is open
- **What happens:** directors already registered can't edit or withdraw; sent invitation links say "closed"
- **Suggested fix:** once registration has opened, the opening day can't move later than today
- **Decision:** _pending_

### 4. The director list scrolls inside a box, hiding ticked people; the question doesn't name them · severity 3 · 3 of 8
- **Suggested fix:** let the list grow with the page; name the recipients in "Send 3 invitations?"
- **Decision:** _pending_

### 5. "Couldn't send · check the address" is a dead end · severity 3 · 6 of 8
- **What happens:** no way to fix the address; the reason is often not the address (plan hold-back, email service down); reminders may go to it again
- **Suggested fix:** "Fix address" on the row; a reason per failure; leave failed addresses out of reminders
- **Decision:** _pending (owner)_

### 6. The tab row is cut off on phones · severity 3 · UI Designer (deferred last review)
- **Decision:** _pending (owner)_

### 7. Close-early button says "Continue" · severity 3 · 6 of 8
- **Suggested fix:** "Close registration", in the danger style
- **Decision:** _pending_

## Should fix (severity 2)
- The server can invite or remind a director who already registered (imported list); one helper for the count on both pages (Software Engineer)
- Directors' page before opening: opening date first; pay button secondary until registration opens; "Add to calendar" (5 of 8)
- After publishing, "Next" should be inviting directors, not the schedule; "Live · 0 of 1 done" (3 of 8)
- Invite tab: the directors list before import; import once (3 of 8)
- Get the word out offers "Invite directors" after registration closes (Software Engineer)
- Big sends (hundreds) can stop part-way with no word (Software Engineer)
- Email wording: "the host's records", "there's still room", the 7-day link in plain words; preview uses a real address (4 of 8)
- "Registration settings, and inviting directors." fragment; "Invitations sent" tab label before any could be sent
- "Close registration" looks different on each page and is small (UI Designer)

## Polish (severity 1)
- Reminder email has no preview; "7 days" hard-coded; unused export; smaller races in sending

## What's working well
- "Publish, registration opens Aug 15" and the "When you publish" list say exactly what happens
- Get the word out is the most prominent thing after publishing, and changes for a first-time host
- The invite list shows invited, couldn't send, and registered in words; registered rows can't be picked
- "Send 3 invitations?" with "Nobody is added to your team"
- The close-early question names the directors who haven't registered
- The opening day is enforced in the database, with tests; every open check reads it

## Limits

This is an AI review, not testing with real people. Before band contests go to real hosts, have two
or three hosts publish one and invite directors.
