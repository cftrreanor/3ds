# Design review: admin Packages page (built, light check)

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Reviewers:** UI Designer, usability consultant · **Mock-ups:** approved by the
owner (`2026-11-11-packages-mockups/`) · **Screenshots:** `2026-11-11-packages-built/`

This is an AI review; the page is for the owner only. Nothing was rated 3 or 4.

## Severity 2 (all fixed)
- Saved and Undo showed at the top of the page, out of view for edits lower down; focus was lost after
  saving. Now a message pinned to the bottom of the screen, and focus goes back to the box changed.
- Save could be pressed before "who this affects" loaded, and a failed check looked like "nobody". Save
  now waits ("Checking…"), a failed check says so with Try again, and it's announced to screen readers.
- Price boxes didn't say their price to screen readers. They do now.
- Save went grey with no reason. Now it says why (team spots of 1 or more, a name, a price).
- Columns hidden on tablet and phone with no cue, and row names scrolled away. Row names stay put, with a
  "Scroll sideways" line.
- Help text far from its field in Name and prices. Moved next to what it explains.

## Severity 1 (taken)
"Not set" prices are muted; "No teacher price" instead of "One rate"; package names look clickable;
pass list narrower with a plain heading; clearer prices note; a clearer disabled Save.

## What's working well
- Every package side by side; each status has a word, not just a color
- "Who this affects" names the accounts and says plainly that nothing is taken away
- Undo, a note for the activity log, and the current setting marked "(now)"
