## The consultant's verdict

Build after fixes. The Registration form itself is clear, forgiving, and accessible. The problems
are around it, in how the event page explains publishing and progress. The one change that would
help most: make "publish" mean one thing everywhere (the page goes live and registration opens),
and put the publish button back at the top once everything is ready.

## Blocks people (severity 3–4)

### 1. Publishing opens registration, but the page says it's a later step · severity 3 · found by 7 of 8
- **Where:** event page when ready (screen 7), the setup list's "After you publish: Open band registration · Optional"; the Band registration card's "Settings, opening registration, and inviting band directors"
- **What happens:** the button says "Publish and open registration", then the list says opening registration is a separate optional job afterward.
- **Who it hurts:** organizer and PTO president, who can't tell whether directors can register yet
- **Standard:** consistency; same thing, same word
- **Suggested fix:** drop "Open band registration" from the list for band contests. Card line: "No bands registered yet. Registration opens when you publish."
- **Decision:** _pending_

### 2. Once ready, the publish button is buried · severity 3 · found by 5 of 8
- **Where:** event page when ready (screen 7), every width
- **What happens:** the "Next step" card at the top disappears once registration is done. "Publish and open registration" is about 1,500 px down, below three other cards.
- **Who it hurts:** organizer, PTO president on a phone, teacher with 30 minutes
- **Standard:** action over information
- **Suggested fix:** keep the Next step card at the top, saying "Ready to publish", with the publish button in it.
- **Decision:** _pending_

### 3. The deadline takes any date, and a slip closes registration · severity 3 · found by 4 of 8
- **Where:** Registration deadline (screens 1, 2, 8)
- **What happens:** the date saves the moment it changes. A date in the past or after the contest is accepted. On a live contest, a slip closes registration for every band at once, with no warning. (Clearing it on a live contest is already refused by the database, but the message then can't be fixed by Retry.)
- **Who it hurts:** organizer; band directors shut out without knowing why
- **Standard:** error prevention
- **Suggested fix:** the database refuses a deadline after the contest date; the page refuses a past date (unless it's unchanged); on a live contest, ask before a change that would close registration; if a save is refused, put back the last saved date.
- **Decision:** _pending_

### 4. Clearing the chaperone limit saves 0 · severity 3 · found by 1 of 8 (Software Engineer)
- **Where:** Chaperone limit per band
- **What happens:** an empty box saves as 0 and says "Saved". Every band that brings chaperones can then no longer save changes to its registration. Lowering the limit below what bands already registered isn't checked either.
- **Who it hurts:** band directors, organizer
- **Standard:** error prevention
- **Suggested fix:** an empty box isn't saved; refuse a limit lower than a band already registered, naming the band.
- **Decision:** _pending_

### 5. "Nobody is emailed" next to "Directors are emailed when you publish" · severity 3 · found by 2 of 8
- **Where:** event page, Performance schedule card vs the publish card
- **What happens:** the schedule card means publishing the *schedule*, but reads as publishing the *event*.
- **Who it hurts:** organizer, who is careful about what goes out
- **Standard:** promises we keep
- **Suggested fix:** "Directors are emailed their times when you publish the schedule."
- **Decision:** _pending_

## Should fix (severity 2)

- **Failed save message:** repeats itself ("Please try again. Retry") and the field's red border is hidden while it has focus. Fix: "Not saved. Check your connection, then select Retry." Keep the red border on focus, add an icon, and make the bar's message jump to the field. (6 of 8)
- **Typed but not added:** "Press Enter" doesn't fit phones, and the note is far from the box. Leaving the box also adds half-typed text. Fix: "“5A” isn't added yet. Select Add to keep it", shown under the box too; don't add on blur or when switching apps, just keep the warning. (6 of 8)
- **Errors while still typing:** a pause mid phone number shows a red error. Fix: check format before saving, show format errors when leaving the box, clear them on the next change. (1 of 8)
- **The bar ignores classification saves:** it can say "Saved" while a classification add failed. Fix: count classification adds and removals in the bar. (1 of 8)
- **Step 1 and step 2 save differently:** Details has a Save button; Registration saves itself; "Next up" is a link on one, a button on the other. (5 of 8)
- **Setup progress disagrees:** "1 of 3 needed" fills the middle segment; "Ready to publish" shows 2 of 3; "Add information for directors" is listed as its own task but is a field on Registration; desktop list is cut off. (5 of 8)
- **Phone sticky setup bar covers buttons** (screens 6, 7). May be how the screenshots were taken; check on a phone. (4 of 8)
- **Classifications can't be renamed** once bands are in them, so a typo is stuck. (2 of 8)
- **Director contact privacy:** withdrawn directors can still see the contact; two co-hosts' first saves can overwrite each other; a failed contact save at publish is ignored. (1 of 8)
- **Registration settings says directors "agree to"** something; there's nothing to agree to. Use the step 2 wording. (3 of 8)
- **Phone tab rows run off the edge** with no cue. (3 of 8)
- **Director page footer** reads like a category label, not our voice. (1 of 8)
- **Paid status** for each band. (2 of 8) _Already decided: no paid tracking; the payment link stays._

## Polish (severity 1)

- Sentence case: "Band Registration", "Event Settings", "Broadcast Message"
- "Optional." under Venue name has a period
- "· 4 classifications" next to the step name is noise
- Registration shows "Now" even when it's complete
- Common-set buttons wrap awkwardly on phones
- The add box's spoken name doesn't include "Classifications"
- "1a" and "1A" are both kept; no cap on how many
- Publishing and opening registration are two saves, not one
- A co-host's edits don't appear until reload
- "Unpublish anytime" vs "at any time"; "Map" vs "Directions"

## What's working well

- Help text in directors' own words, with reasons ("so directors have their times in time to book buses")
- Common-set shortcuts, and the contact filled in with a clear note on who sees it
- Failed saves show at the field and in the bar, in words, with a 44 px Retry
- Classifications with bands show their counts and explain why they can't be removed
- The not-ready publish card names exactly what's missing, with one button to fix it
- "When you publish" is honest and calm ("Nobody is emailed", "unpublish at any time")
- The directors' page leads with the deadline, the information, and the pay button
- The rules live in the database, with tests; the payment link is https only
- Dark mode holds up on every screen

## By role

- **Product Manager:** the step asks only what directors need; the weak point is the moment of publishing (buried button, two progress systems). Raised paid tracking (already decided against).
- **Product Owner:** deadline rules and the checklist contradiction; wants states not yet photographed (closed registration on the directors' page, co-host without edit rights).
- **UX Designer:** a live deadline change closes registration silently; failed saves should take you to the field.
- **UI Designer:** opened all 60 shots; the failed field isn't visibly marked while focused; tab rows and the setup list clip.
- **Software Engineer:** only reviewer to find the chaperone limit, errors while typing, half-typed classifications being added, and the contact privacy gaps.
- **UX Content Manager:** the publish story, the repetitive error message, "Press Enter", "agree to".
- **Marketing Manager:** two opposite promises about who gets emailed; the directors' page footer is a missed chance.
- **Consultant:** build after fixes; the form is strong, the surroundings need one story.

## Acceptance criteria (from the Product Owner)

- Given no classification or deadline, when I open Publish, then the publish button is hidden and "Go to registration" takes me to step 2.
- Given a field fails to save, when the connection returns and I select Retry, then I see "Saved" and the bar clears.
- Given a deadline after the contest date, when I pick it, then I see an error next to the field and it isn't saved.
- Given a live contest, when I try to clear the deadline, then I'm stopped and the last saved date stays.
- Given a classification has bands, then its remove button is gone and a reason is shown.
- Given a payment link, directors see "Pay the entry fee"; given none, no button shows.
- Given publishing opens registration, then no checklist item asks me to open it again.

## Limits

This is an AI review, not testing with real people. For anything rated 3 or 4, try it with two
or three real people from the six groups before launch.
