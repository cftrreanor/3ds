# Field Command Events: Tone and Voice Rules

Use these rules for all website and in-app copy.

## Brand foundation

- **Anchor words:** organized and supported
- **Tagline:** *Every detail organized. Every organizer supported.* (use exactly as written)
- **Brand statement:** Field Command Events keeps events organized, so facilitators can focus on their people, not the logistics. And when you need help, you'll reach a real person, not a bot.
- **Personality source:** the founder's reputation for getting stuff done, being reliable, helpful, and kind, and listening

## Voice traits

- **Clear, not clever:** short sentences, plain words, and the answer first
- **Calm and steady:** no hype, no exclamation points, and no dramatizing chaos
- **Warm, not cute:** kind and human, but professional
- **Shows we listen:** the audience's own words, not generic software language
- **Promises we keep:** specific claims only, never overpromising

## Writing mechanics

- Professional tone throughout
- No comma splices
- Always the Oxford comma
- Few, powerful words for each point
- No periods on incomplete sentences in headlines, buttons, bullets, or labels
- Question marks stay on questions
- The tagline is the only exception to the period rule

## Word choice

- **Tagline:** "organizer"
- **Brand statement:** "facilitators"
- **Administrators page:** "facilitator" when naming the role
- **Everywhere else:** "organizer" when naming the role
- **Body copy:** "you" preferred over either word when speaking to the reader
- **In-app role names:** "Host", "Co-host", and "Account Host" stay as the names of the account permission levels inside the app. Use "organizer" on the website and when describing the person in general copy.
- **Sign-up:** "sign-up" is the noun ("Sign-up open", "your sign-up"), "sign up" is the verb ("Sign up to volunteer"). Never "signup".

## Audiences

The core voice never changes. Only the leading details shift.

- **Band and choir directors:** practical and specific, proving it works on contest day
- **Teachers running specialized events** (theater, UIL academics, fairs): welcoming and jargon-free, showing it fits their event
- **Classroom teachers** (parties, parent-teacher conferences, campus visits): quick and light, focused on time saved and ease for parents
- **Administrators:** reassuring and concrete, focused on reliability, support, and less staff strain

## Website vs. in-app

- **Website:** written for the people who decide
- **In-app:** brief, direct, and calm for everyone using it on the day

## Support promise

"Supported" is a system, not a person on call.

- **The product supports first:** clear setup, in-app guidance, and event-day screens that prevent questions
- **Self-serve help:** short guides and answers for common questions
- **Real people behind it:** humans, not bots, with honest response times (we promise a reply within a day)
- **Extra care where it matters:** priority help for big events, possibly in a higher pricing tier

## Claims guardrails

- Promise real people, not instant or constant availability
- State response times honestly, and only ones we can keep
- Never promise "always," "24/7," or "anytime" support
- Avoid "privacy protected" until data practices are confirmed
- Use "no ads, no spam, emails used only for your event" instead
- Name frustrations with other tools, never the competitors themselves

## Navigation and structure

- Label paths by the task people are organizing, not their job title
- Administrators are the exception and are named directly
- The homepage routes and the specialized pages sell
- Every page ends with a clear next step, so no path is a dead end

## Homepage structure

1. **Hero**
   - Headline: *Every detail organized. Every organizer supported.*
   - Supporting line: *Contests, school events, and simple sign-ups, with real people behind every one*
   - Primary button: *Join the pilot*

2. **Router: "What are you organizing?"** (four cards, each linking to its page)
   - **Contests and festivals:** Schedules, rooms, judges, and buses in one place
   - **School events:** Theater, UIL academics, fairs, and more
   - **Classroom sign-ups:** Parties, conferences, and visits, ready in minutes
   - **For administrators:** Reliable events with less strain on your staff

3. **Built for the people running school events** (three points)
   - **Organized:** sign-ups, schedules, and check-in in one place
   - **Supported:** guided setup, and real people, not bots, for the rest
   - **Respectful:** no account for families, volunteers, or visitors, and no ads or spam

4. **Classroom sign-ups**
   - Headline: *Sign-ups that respect everyone's time*
   - Supporting line: *Classroom parties, parent-teacher conferences, and campus visits on one simple page*
   - **One form for the whole family:** register every adult and every student at once
   - **One page, done:** no clicking through screen after screen
   - **No ads, no spam:** emails used for your event and nothing else

5. **How support works**
   - Describe the support system above: guidance built into the product, quick answers for common questions, and real people when you need them
   - No phone numbers, response-time promises, or "always available" language unless confirmed

6. **Closing call to action**
   - Primary: *Join the pilot*
   - Secondary: *Talk with us*
